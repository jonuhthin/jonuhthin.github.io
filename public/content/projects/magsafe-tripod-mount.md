A tripod compatible adapter for using a wireless charging puck as a phone mount on a tripod.

Printed in Clear PLA with concentric infill to show off the internals a bit. Needs:
- 1/4-20 nut x 1
- M3 x 10 bolts x 4
- Charging puck x 1 
  - I used [this one](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DLP157QH?ref_=ppx_hzsearch_conn_dt_b_fed_asin_title_8)
